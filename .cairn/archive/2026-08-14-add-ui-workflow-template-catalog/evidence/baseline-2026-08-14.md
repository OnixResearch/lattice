## Boundaries retained

- Existing `NodeTemplate` values remain shell-node templates.
- Workflow-template selection must not write the store or start orchestration.
- Existing workflow and orchestration parsers and admission stay authoritative.
- The loader must not retrieve network content or execute bundle content.
- Content identities prove exact local bytes only. They do not prove behavior, correctness, authority, or release readiness.
