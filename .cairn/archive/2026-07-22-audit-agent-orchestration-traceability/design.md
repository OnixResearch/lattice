# Design

The audit partitions the exact 39-ID agent-orchestration subset. Direct scenario assertions are exact; rows lacking complete inert-output, overage, actionable status, remote-exactly-once non-claim, external-anchor non-claim, or hidden-cycle assertions remain partial. Immutable source snapshots preserve byte identity.

Positive and adversarial validation rejects missing or unexpected decisions, classification/rationale/path drift, stale evidence hashes, selected markers, or any coverage drift.
