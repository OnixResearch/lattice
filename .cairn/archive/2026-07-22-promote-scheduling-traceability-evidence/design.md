# Design: promote scheduling traceability evidence

## Exact delta

The baseline contains 324 missing IDs. The result MUST contain 317 and remove exactly the seven completed scheduling IDs. No unrelated ID may be removed or added.

## Local bindings

Markers attach only to direct tests. Composite requirements may attach to multiple tests where no single test covers every clause. History binds terminal durable history; compatibility binds full plan equality; listener startup binds the real process/socket test; cookbook flow binds import/dry-run, accepted, and rejected paths.

## Historical identity

Completion evidence remains byte-pinned. Historical checks remove only the expected marker-only lines before hashing current test sources. Audit evidence already uses preserved pre-completion source snapshots.

## Validation

Deterministic promotion validation checks sorted unique sets, exact removal, no additions, zero dangling markers, unsuccessful coverage, every local binding, and policy exclusion, with positive and negative cases.
