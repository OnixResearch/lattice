UCAN boundary. Distinct operation resources, explicit trusted roots, real holder
verification, opaque zeroizing sessions and non-consuming current revalidation

## Acceptance boundary

Native validation, proposal/design/tasks gates, sync/archive and post-archive
validation are separate lifecycle observations and will be retained separately.
Neither a passing test nor native gate permits release, grants a real issuer,
establishes revocation freshness, proves external resolver correctness or proves
that any Mantle operation executed. Basalt maintainers own this contract and
its repository-owned repeatability rail.
