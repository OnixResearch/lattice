# Design

The immutable completion receipt is the 222-item baseline. Promotion markers bind only to maintained tests whose current or historically captured bytes support the completed exact decisions. Multi-scenario parent requirements may repeat across the directly relevant tests.

A pure set comparator requires baseline minus current to equal the 41-ID allowlist, current minus baseline to be empty, missing coverage to equal 181, dangling markers to remain empty, and coverage to remain unsuccessful. Binding, policy, duplicate, and unrelated-set drift are rejected adversarially.
